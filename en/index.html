From archive to answer

Document work that can explain itself.

Sovinity connects existing repositories to private AI. The original document remains the evidence — not the answer that merely sounds convincing.

Connect

Local files, Nextcloud/WebDAV and email attachments become one knowledge layer. Existing archives do not have to move to someone else's cloud first.

Understand

OCR, semantic search and local language models make scanned records discoverable. Answers lead back to the file, page, slide or spreadsheet.

Organise

Sovinity suggests filenames, folders and clean-up work. Changes can be approved, audited and undone.

Data sovereignty by architecture

Private AI is more than a promise.

Sovinity is designed for local or isolated installations. Files, extracted text, vectors and AI models can stay in an environment you control. Where data is processed should be visible and verifiable.

Questions

Private means verifiable.

Must documents be sent to a public AI service?

No. The architecture supports local models through Ollama. Any future managed offer should state clearly where content is stored and processed.

Does Sovinity replace a full document-management system?

Not initially. Sovinity is a private intelligence and filing layer over existing files, Nextcloud and email attachments. It makes archives more useful without immediately replacing every specialist system.

Is Sovinity available now?

A working MVP exists. The public launch and licensing model have not yet been set; target groups and pilot scope are currently being validated.

Does this website track visitors?

We do not add analytics, marketing services or external font services to this static website. Hosting details for GitHub Pages are described in the privacy notice.
